# Event: London AI x Science Hackathon

Back to [README](../README.md) · See also: [application draft](application-draft.md)

From the public event pages (<https://luma.com/3iipivod>, <https://iterate.inc/london-ai-science>),
fetched 2026-10-03. Re-check them before relying on these facts.

- **Organisers:** Iterate with futurebio, part of London Deep Tech Week
- **Format:** 48-hour hackathon, 3–4 Oct 2026, London, in person
- **Theme:** applying frontier AI to challenging scientific problems, including "autonomous
  science, novel materials discovery, decoding complex biological systems and more"
- **Teams:** 2–5 people
- **Tracks:** AI automated discovery of algorithms (Cambridge Compute Co., AIDDA Institute) ·
  Originator (agents that do science and know when they're wrong or reward hacking) ·
  drug and protein design (Serova) · materials manufacturing (Polaron)
- **Prizes:** grand prize $1,500 + credits · $850 per track + credits · $100 for the most viral
  post
- **Judging:** five equal criteria: technicality, creativity, usefulness, demo, track/sponsor
  alignment
