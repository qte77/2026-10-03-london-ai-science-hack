# London AI x Science Hackathon (2026-10-03)

Notes and application for the AI x Science Hackathon in London, organised by Iterate with
futurebio as part of London Deep Tech Week.

- Event: <https://luma.com/3iipivod> · <https://iterate.inc/london-ai-science>

## Event facts

From the two event pages above, fetched 2026-10-03. Re-check them before relying on these.

- **Format:** 48-hour hackathon, 3–4 Oct 2026, London, in person
- **Theme:** applying frontier AI to challenging scientific problems, including "autonomous
  science, novel materials discovery, decoding complex biological systems and more"
- **Teams:** 3–5 people
- **Tracks:** computer-use agents (Originator) · autoresearch for discovery / open maths problems
  (Cambridge Compute Co., AIDDA Institute) · drug and protein design (Serova) · materials
  manufacturing (Polaron)
- **Prizes:** grand prize $1,500 + credits · $850 per track + credits · $100 for the most viral
  post
- **Judging:** teams pitch to judges, VCs and researchers. No rubric was published.
